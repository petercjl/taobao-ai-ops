# Category Opportunity Judgment

## Intent and evidence boundary

Start from a familiar 一级类目 while looking beyond the seller's existing assortment. Always offer large, medium, and medium-small market research routes so users can choose different market sizes. A large growing market may have strong brands; a smaller market may also be highly concentrated. Category monthly totals cannot establish white-label accessibility. This scope and simultaneous-route requirement are user-confirmed. `U`

## Read the market before ranking

Use 24 completed months: latest 12 vs preceding 12, corresponding-month growth counts, and latest six vs the same six last year. Examine first/second-level scale, increment and share shifts, plus third-level distribution and hierarchy reconciliation. Withhold unsupported growth on missing or zero-base data. One 24-month series supports one full-year comparison, not a proven multiyear trajectory. `U/E`

## Relative capacity with category-specific review

Define the population as complete, positive-recent-GMV third-level paths under the same first-level category, preserving full taxonomy paths. Record count, median, p10/p90, top-decile share of this population's GMV, missingness and ties. This measures category-size distribution, not seller or brand concentration. Broad administrative categories and narrow product categories may not be equally granular; name that limitation. `A/E`

Start provisionally at P40/P70/P90: below P40 watch; P40–P70 medium-small; P70–P90 medium; P90+ large. These are reviewable relative positions, not confirmed universal business rules. The current category's observed distribution supplies monetary boundaries. Consider taxonomy granularity, skew, sparse/tied data, sensitivity and the user's sales ambitions when reviewing the positions. Record why they were retained or adjusted. Do not claim statistical adaptivity establishes optimal commercial bands. `A`

Quantiles use linear interpolation of sorted observed annual amounts. Same amounts stay in one band; exact boundary values enter the higher band. Small populations and coincident cutoffs can create empty routes: show them rather than force candidates. Show ±5-point joint quantile-shift sensitivity. Exclude incomplete and nonpositive observations from the quantile population, keeping them in the full table. A lower boundary prevents relative tail selection but does not guarantee enough money for a business; evaluate target revenue divided by category size later as a required-share scenario, not a share forecast. `A`

## Independent research routes

All routes first require complete data, calculable growth, both growth floors and persistent positive months. Reviewable defaults: annual and latest-six-month YoY each at least 20%, at least 10 positive corresponding months. Mixed “其他/其它” classes require decomposition and remain visible outside ranked pools. `A`

- Large-market increment: rank by 100 × max(annual increment,0) / root latest annual GMV × positive months / 12. Retains the original aim of finding substantial new demand.
- Medium-capacity sustained growth: rank by 100 × min(annual YoY, latest-six-month YoY) × positive months / 12.
- Medium-small-capacity growth: same strength metric, independently ranked in its own capacity band.

The latter metric gives no extra reward for capacity once the band is satisfied. It is still sensitive to base effects. Annual and half-year periods overlap and are not independent confirmations. All scores are transparent heuristics for research ordering, not profit forecasts or scientific success probabilities. Tie-break on positive months then full path. Output the complete pool and top N per route (default six); never mix different metrics into one global top list. Keep a largest-market benchmark and measured watch/defer counterexamples. `U/A`

## Growth diagnostics and next evidence

Compare first-half and second-half YoY within the recent year to describe acceleration/deceleration; a provisional 3-percentage-point tolerance labels similar speeds. Show top-three positive monthly increments divided by all positive monthly increments; high concentration asks for seasonal/source verification, not automatic rejection. Never turn missing histories into zero or claim a newly observed taxonomy path is a new consumer market. `A/E`

Before deep product research, perform a separately authorized lightweight competition check: product/shop rankings, brand grouping, non-leading sellers' turnover, price bands and sampled coverage. Ranking-sample brand shares do not equal whole-market shares; seller and brand concentration are different concepts. Then investigate demand, product differences, suppliers and full economics. Monthly category data cannot answer those questions. `U/W`

## Reading-first report

Every table is paired with a selectable chart of the same evidence. Preserve exact values in the table and point/bar tooltips; use chronological lines for monthly data, grouped zero-based bars for category comparisons, and separate unit/metric groups rather than misleading shared axes. Charts must follow table filtering and ordering; long lists disclose pagination. Missing data creates gaps, not zeroes. This display requirement is user-confirmed. `U`

Open with three concise data-grounded market judgments and all route summaries. Each route shows its relative band and actual amount boundaries, population/pool/shortlist funnel, distinct metric, and linked candidates. Put full formulas and distribution review in disclosure. Preserve all hierarchy tables, full per-route shortlists, individual submenu pages with 12 month-matched comparisons, benchmark, watch/defer and one searchable, filterable, numerically sortable full third-level table including incomplete paths and exclusion reasons. Selective color, bold and italic support numbered explanations. `U`
